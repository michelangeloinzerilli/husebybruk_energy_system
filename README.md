# Future Energy Systems Analysis at Huseby Bruk

Project developed within the **EIT InnoEnergy SELECT Master's programme** for Huseby Bruk, a historic property managed by the **Swedish National Property Board (SFV)**, with project guidance from **SWECO**.

## Overview

This project investigates how the energy system of **Huseby Bruk, Sweden**, could evolve toward greater energy self-sufficiency, resilience and sustainability while reducing dependence on the external electricity grid.

A key challenge is integrating new renewable energy technologies while respecting the site's cultural heritage, existing infrastructure and seasonal mismatch between local energy production and demand.

## Scope

The study evaluates several potential solutions for the future energy system, including:

- **Solar PV**, with a focus on solar carports
- **Bioenergy and biogas production**
- **Small-scale wind generation**
- **Hydropower**
- **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)**
- **Virtual and local grid concepts**

The different technologies were assessed from technical, economic and practical perspectives, including their compatibility with the historical character of the property.

## Methodology

The project combines **energy system analysis** and **techno-economic assessment**.

The main activities included:

- Analysis and processing of historical energy consumption and renewable generation data
- Development of hourly energy profiles from available datasets
- Solar PV system design and production estimation
- Wind-resource and small-scale turbine assessment
- Biogas production and anaerobic digester sizing
- Battery storage sizing across different time scales
- CAPEX, OPEX and economic feasibility analysis
- Assessment of alternative local electricity-network configurations
- Integration of different renewable technologies into future energy-system scenarios
